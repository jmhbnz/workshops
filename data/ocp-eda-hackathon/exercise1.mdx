Welcome to the *Red Hat OpenShift Event Driven Automation Hackathon*.

In this hackathon you'll explore how to use [Ansible Automation Platform](https://www.redhat.com/en/technologies/management/ansible) to automatically respond to platform events ocurring in [OpenShift](https://www.redhat.com/en/technologies/cloud-computing/openshift).

**Let's get started!**


## 1.1 - The scenario

For this hackathon, we're going to be an OpenShift team member at **ACME Financial Services**, a large bank operating in New Zealand. They run a significant number of customer facing workloads on OpenShift and have a small core team of platform engineers supporting the platform.

![acme](/workshops/static/images/fsi-security/acme.jpg)

Throughout this hackathon we'll be in the shoes of Sarah, a senior OpenShift Platform Engineer at ACME, a day in the life for her typically sees her handling platform health and maintenance tasks, assisting application teams with feature requests and she is also rostered on to the platform incident management queue on a regular rotation.

![acme](/workshops/static/images/fsi-security/sarah-desk.png)


## 1.2 - Understanding the environment

For this hackathon you'll be given access to the ACME Financial Services Production OpenShift `4.21` cluster and Ansible Automation Platform `2.7`.


![cluster](/workshops/static/images/fsi-security/acme-cluster.png)
 *ACME Financial Services OpenShift Web Console*



## 1.3 - Obtain your environment

To get underway open your web browser and navigate to [https://catalog.demo.redhat.com/workshop/cjjhdp](https://catalog.demo.redhat.com/workshop/cjjhdp) to allocate a hackathon environment for your team.

Once the page loads you can login with any email address and the password provided by your hackathon facilitator.


![Workshop login](/workshops/static/images/ocp-aap/workshop-login.png)
 *Login to obtain a hackathon environment*


After logging in you will be presented with access details for OpenShift and Ansible Automation Platform. Use credentials on that page to log in to the OpenShift Web Console now.

If you have an environment and are logged in to the OpenShift Web Console, you're now ready to move on to [Track Selection](/workshop/exercise2), let the challenge begin! 🎉
